About iButton Console

A browser console for Maxim/Analog iButton temperature and humidity loggers (DS1921, DS1922, DS1923, DS1925). It reads and configures loggers through a DS9490R USB reader over WebUSB, and it can open CSV files and earlier reads. Everything runs in your browser; no data is sent anywhere.
